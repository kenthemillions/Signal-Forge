Understanding Signals

Here's what each signal state means for your trading.

WAIT
Do nothing. Market conditions aren't clear or price isn't at a good location. Patience is profitable.
PREPARE
Get ready. Price is approaching a zone with some conditions met. Watch for confirmation triggers.
BUY
Bullish setup confirmed. All criteria met—structure, confirmation, and volume aligned for longs.
SELL
Bearish setup confirmed. All criteria met—structure, confirmation, and volume aligned for shorts/puts.
